# US pad mounted transformer manufacturers (UL XPLH)

Every company holding a UL listing under category XPLH to build liquid filled pad mounted distribution
transformers for the US market. 172 companies across 24 countries (8 with country unstated), with country of manufacture, whether that
country is TAA eligible, and the UL file number so you can check each listing yourself.

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Companies | 172 |
| China | 71 |
| United States | 39 |
| South Korea | 13 |

**File:** [`us-pad-mounted-transformer-manufacturers.csv`](us-pad-mounted-transformer-manufacturers.csv)

**Columns:** `company`, `country`, `country_code`, `taa_eligible_country` (yes or no, by country of manufacture
under the Trade Agreements Act designated country list), `ul_file` (UL category and file number), `source_page`.

## Why this exists

A UL listing means a company is permitted to build these for the US market. It does not mean it holds stock or
will quote a single unit. The full list, with which makers hold inventory, lead times and delivered pricing
notes, is kept at **[Padmount Supply, every UL listed pad mounted transformer manufacturer](https://padmountsupply.com/transformer-manufacturers)**,
and current lead times are tracked monthly at
**[Transformer lead times](https://padmountsupply.com/guides/transformer-lead-times)**.

## Source and updates

Compiled by [Padmount Supply](https://padmountsupply.com) from UL Product iQ listings under XPLH. Checked
November 2026. Listings change; confirm a file number in UL Product iQ before relying on it.
